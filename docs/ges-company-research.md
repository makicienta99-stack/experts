# GES (Global Experience Specialists): Company Research & M&A Profile

*Research compiled: November 26, 2026 · Website: [ges.com](https://www.ges.com)*

> **Why this doc exists:** GES is actively buying companies. Under its private-equity owner, Truelink Capital, it has closed two add-on acquisitions in about 11 months. This brief covers who GES is, how it is built, what it has bought and why, and where it is likely to buy next. It also lists the requirements for a future M&A application that would support that pipeline.

## 2. What GES Does: Business Lines

GES is a group of companies that serve two buyers: **event organizers** (associations and show producers) and **brand marketers / exhibitors**.

| Brand / Unit | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **GES Exhibitions** | Official general services contractor (GSC) for trade shows: floor plans, booth build, freight and material handling, logistics, rentals, graphics, labor | The historic core. Lower margin, high volume, and sticky multi-year show contracts |
| **Spiro** | Global experiential agency: custom exhibits, brand activations, corporate events. Official Exhibitor-Appointed Contractor (EAC) in the GES family | Historically the higher-margin unit. Acquired 2Heads in Dec 2025 |
| **onPeak** | Event housing and hotel room-block management | Now part of the new **"Event Intelligence"** group |
| **Visit by GES** (visitcloud.com) | Event registration, badging and attendee engagement tech | Part of **"Event Intelligence"** |
| **SHOWTECH** | Power, lighting and electrical services (Canada) | |
| **CSG Creative** | Marketing and creative agency for associations and events: audience growth and attendance marketing | **Acquired Nov 2026**. Runs as a standalone operating company |
| **2Heads** | London creative agency with offices in LA and Montreal: design-led brand experiences | **Acquired Dec 2025** through Spiro |

**Rebrand to watch:** In Nov 2026, GES renamed its "Tech-Enabled Services" group to **"Event Intelligence"** (led by Lisa Baez). The group is built on onPeak and Visit, and the stated aim is to combine "technology, connected data, AI and human expertise." This points to future buys in event data, AI and attendee tech.

Recent positioning language: *"a global live events company providing exhibition services, brand experiences, and live event venue services."* The phrase "live event venue services" is new and may signal a move into in-venue services (exclusive electrical, AV or similar contracts at convention centers).

**Verticals served:** healthcare, aerospace and defense, financial services, manufacturing, technology, retail, associations.

**Key clients named in public sources:** Informa, MHA, IMTS, MINExpo, IAAPA, Global Pet Expo, NATIA, Boys & Girls Clubs of America, AUSA (Eurosatory 2026 pavilion). CSG adds WEFTEC, NSSF/SHOT Show, NTCA, AARP, American Chemical Society, ISA Sign Expo and SIOR.

## 3. Financial Profile (last public figures)

GES has been private since Dec 31, 2024, so the last audited figures come from Viad's SEC filings.

|---|---|---|---|
| Total GES revenue | $828.0M | **$888.4M** (+7.3%) | n/a |
| Spiro revenue | $277.6M | n/a | n/a |
| GES Exhibitions revenue | $557.9M | n/a | n/a |
| Total GES Adj. EBITDA | $61.3M | **$68.2M** | **$85M–$95M** |
| Adj. EBITDA margin | ~7.4% | ~7.7% | ~10% (implied) |

- **Deal valuation:** $535M / ~$90M midpoint 2024 Adj. EBITDA = **about 5.9x EBITDA**. That is a low, carve-out-style multiple, which leaves Truelink room to create value through add-ons and margin expansion.
- **Spiro carries higher margins** (~9.7% Adj. EBITDA margin in 2022) than GES Exhibitions (low-to-mid single digits in many quarters). This helps explain why the first two add-ons went into agency and creative services.
- In 2022, Viad sold a non-core audio-visual business (ON Services, ~$50–74M revenue). GES has exited AV before, so any AV acquisition would be a reversal of strategy.
- **Revenue is lumpy.** Show rotation (non-annual mega-shows such as IMTS and MINExpo) moves revenue by about $30M a year.

## 4. Ownership: Truelink Capital

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Firm | Truelink Capital, a Los Angeles middle-market PE firm |
| Founded | May 2022 by **Luke Myers** and **Todd Golditch** (both ex-Platinum Equity managing directors) |
| Fund | Truelink Capital I LP, about **$875M** |
| Playbook | Corporate carve-outs, operational improvement and **"transformative add-on acquisitions"** |
| Focus sectors | Industrials, tech-enabled services, B2B, transportation and logistics |
| Other named team | Sam Handler (posted on the 2Heads deal) |

**What this means for M&A:** Platinum Equity alumni run a **carve-out, operate, buy-and-build** playbook. Expect the following:
- A steady stream of **bolt-ons**: small to mid-size, founder-owned, and often kept under their own brand.
- Deals that **raise the blended margin** (agency, creative, tech, data) or **add geography** (EMEA).
- Typical PE hold of **4–6 years**, which points to an exit around 2029–2031. The goal is to grow EBITDA and exit at a higher multiple than the ~5.9x entry.

Luke Myers at close: *"…drive strategic investments to bolster GES and Spiro's strengths in creative design, logistics and event production, accelerate its tech-enabled offerings, and expand GES' international reach."* This is effectively a public statement of the M&A thesis: **creative, logistics, production, tech and international.**

## 5. Acquisition History

### 5a. Under Truelink (active buy-and-build)

| # | Date | Target | Bought through | Target profile | Strategic rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dec 2, 2025** | **2Heads** | Spiro | London creative and experiential agency, 40+ years old, offices in London, LA and Montreal. Tech, financial-services and consumer brand clients. MD: Paul Godwin | Expands Spiro's creative footprint in the **UK and Europe** and deepens strategic and digital capability |
| 2 | **Nov 5, 2026** | **CSG Creative** | GES (standalone opco) | ~25-year-old association and event marketing agency, carved out of National Trade Productions (NTP). Founder and President Paige Cardwell reports to the GES CEO | Adds **attendee and audience marketing**, so GES covers more of the organizer's event lifecycle (build the audience, then build the show) |

Pricing was **not disclosed** for either deal.

**Pattern so far:**
1. **Small, founder-led agencies.** Teams and brands are kept and leaders stay on.
2. **Capability expansion, not consolidation of competitors** so far. Both targets add services that GES then cross-sells into its existing organizer and brand relationships.
3. **Carve-outs welcome.** CSG was bought from a parent company (NTP), which mirrors how Truelink bought GES itself.
4. **Cadence: roughly one deal a year so far.** It may speed up as integration capacity grows.

### 5b. Organic expansion (signals where M&A could follow)
- **Mar 2026: Paris office and facility** opened (led by Thibault Mervaillie). France is called a cornerstone market for international events.
- Recent new operations in **Qatar** and **India**, and continued build-out of the **Dubai** office.
- **Nov 2026:** "Event Intelligence" rebrand of the tech group.

### 5c. Historical (Viad era, for context)
- GES and Spiro were assembled under Viad over decades. onPeak, Visit and SHOWTECH were folded in, and the Spiro brand consolidated agency assets.
- 2022: Sold the ON Services AV business.

## 6. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Ownership | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Freeman** | Private (family-owned, Dallas) | Market leader in North American GSC and AV. Has historically been a serial acquirer (Champion, Immersa Marketing, Sparks in 2023) |
| **Shepard** | Private (Atlanta) | National GSC, founded 1905. Trade shows, corporate events and exhibits |
| **Fern Expo** | **MSouth Equity Partners** (PE) | Combined with sister company **Nth Degree**. Services 1,400–1,500+ events a year in 200+ cities. A PE-backed rival running its own roll-up |
| **The Expo Group** | Private (Dallas) | Acquired the 1898-founded firm Brede/Allied in 2020 |
| Regional GSCs and EACs | Mostly founder-owned | Hundreds of regional decorators, exhibit houses and freight and logistics firms. **These are the main pool of acquisition targets** |

**Industry dynamics:**
- North American GSC work is concentrated among Freeman and GES, with Shepard and Fern behind them. A long tail of regional players remains.
- PE is consolidating the space (Truelink with GES, MSouth with Fern and Nth Degree), and competition for good targets will grow.
- Live events have fully recovered from COVID. Organizers want **audience growth, data and ROI proof**, which explains the buying focus on marketing, data and AI.

## 9. Risks and Considerations for GES's M&A Program

- **Integration bandwidth.** A ~2,600-person organization adding agencies with different cultures. GES so far keeps acquired brands standalone, which preserves talent but delays synergies.
- **Margin mix.** The core GSC business is thin-margin and labor-intensive (union labor in many U.S. venues). Add-ons need to lift blended margin.
- **Cyclicality.** Live events are exposed to recessions, corporate marketing budget cuts and pandemics. Inflation was still elevated in late 2026, which pressures exhibitor budgets.
- **Leverage.** PE ownership typically means acquisition debt, so deal sizing must fit debt covenants.
- **Client concentration and show rotation.** Large shows (IMTS, MINExpo) rotate, and losing a top show contract can matter.
- **Competition for targets** from Freeman, which is cash-rich, and MSouth/Fern, which is also PE-backed and rolling up.

## 10. Implications for an M&A Application for GES

GES needs to repeat a buy-and-build process with a lean corporate development team. An application could support each stage:

| Stage | Pain point | App feature ideas |
|---|---|---|
| **Sourcing** | Finding hundreds of small, founder-owned agencies, GSCs and event-tech firms worldwide | Target database with filters (category, geography, revenue, headcount, founder age, client list), web and news enrichment, "looks like 2Heads/CSG" similarity scoring |
| **Screening and prioritization** | Ranking fit against the thesis | Fit scorecard: strategic fit, margin accretion, cross-sell potential, geography, culture. Weighted scoring tied to Truelink's thesis (creative, logistics, production, tech, international) |
| **Pipeline / CRM** | Tracking outreach and conversations | Kanban pipeline: Identified → Contacted → NDA → IOI → LOI → Diligence → Closed/Dead, with owners, next steps and reminders |
| **Valuation** | Quick back-of-envelope math | EBITDA-multiple calculator, accretion and dilution versus the ~5.9x platform entry multiple, synergy estimates (cross-sell into GES organizer and brand base) |
| **Diligence** | Document chaos | Diligence checklists by target type (agency vs. GSC vs. SaaS), data-room tracker, red-flag log |
| **Integration (post-close)** | Keeping acquired brands standalone while capturing synergies | 100-day plan templates, cross-sell tracker (e.g., CSG services sold to GES organizers), retention tracker for key people |
| **Reporting** | Updates for the board and the PE sponsor | Dashboard of pipeline value, deals by stage, and closed-deal performance versus the original thesis |

**Seed data available now:** the two closed deals (2Heads, CSG), the competitor list in Section 6, and the target categories in Section 7.
